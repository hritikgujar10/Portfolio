Concept campaignInstagram · 2026

Social Media Campaign: Gujar & Sons Cashews

A 7-week Instagram campaign for a family-owned cashew brand from the Konkan: 3 posts a week, 21 posts in all.

Role
Strategy, content & design
Focus
Brand storytelling
Length
7 weeks · 3 posts a week
Goal

Introduce a new, family-run cashew brand from Shrivardhan to Instagram and build an early following that trusts where its snacks come from, ahead of the online store launch.

Target audience

Health-conscious urban snackers aged 22–40 in Mumbai and Pune who buy premium dry fruit online, plus festive-season gift buyers.

Starting point

The brand already posts on Instagram. This campaign plans 7 weeks of content, 3 posts a week, built on that existing feed and its look.

Reels idea (concept)

"Farm to pack in 30 seconds." A POV reel that follows one cashew from the tree to the pouch: picking, sun-drying, sorting and sealing, with on-screen text for each step and a close-up crunch at the end. It closes on the four flavours and a launch-week offer, and doubles as the pinned post.

Capstone projectMS in Digital Marketing · Sacred Heart University

Reigniting Growth for Bigelow Benefits

A 12-month, $50K plan to reposition Bigelow's Benefits wellness teas as the taste-first wellness tea for Gen-Z.

Team
Group project
Focus
Positioning, social & Amazon growth
Tools
RivalIQ, Excel, Meta & TikTok Ads (plan)
The challenge

The Benefits line had no clearly defined target segment, which led to low awareness and weak brand positioning. Gen-Z doesn't think of tea as a wellness tool, and competitors lead with wellness, leaving taste untapped.

My part
  • Conducted market research and competitive analysis to determine strategies for content and product positioning
  • Examined structured datasets to reveal trends in customer behavior and insights on segmentation
  • Assessed product content, metadata and the user journey to pinpoint areas for optimization
  • Designed customer journey maps and KPI forecasts to support the growth plan
Brand strategySocial mediaAmazon SEOInfluencers
The big idea

“Sips Don’t Lie.”

Benefits for the taste-first wellness generation.

Budget breakdown · $50,000
Influencer partnerships$20K · 40%
TikTok Ads$12K · 24%
Social media mgmt & analytics$10K · 20%
Instagram Reels$8K · 16%
Where we can win
0.3% vs 1.4%Instagram engagement versus the category average
No TikTokwhile competitors grew +600% on TikTok with storytelling
15–20%revenue lift brands see from TikTok Shop, with weak Amazon visibility today

Sources: RivalIQ competitive analysis, WSJ, Appeal Wizards

The strategy: from sip to sale
1. Flavor-first storytellingReels and TikTok built on taste, seasonality, tea as a caffeine replacement and wellness rituals.
2. Social proofTwo lifestyle and wellness creators known for authentic self-care and “day in my life” content.
3. Visibility & conversionTikTok Shop for first sales, shoppable IG and Meta ads, then Amazon to capture the demand.
Owning the Amazon search results
  • Target high-search, low-competition keywords
  • Update A+ content to match Gen-Z wellness trends
  • Encourage verified reviews to build trust and ranking
  • Track CTR, add-to-cart rate and sales uplift

Top 3 listings receive 60% of clicks (JungleScout).

Timeline to long-term growth
  1. Months 1–2Launch flavor-led, seasonal TikTok and Reels content; Prime influencer launch
  2. Months 3–5Begin influencer partnerships to strengthen credibility
  3. Months 6–8Strengthen TikTok Shop visibility through promotions
  4. Months 9–12Evaluate campaign performance and use the insights to keep growing
